## How do I choose not to intervene? {#no-intervention}

At an optional Essence Wheel, don't select an Essence—just advance the text. There is no separate “Pass” button.

## Why won't some Wheels let me continue without choosing? {#locked-wheel}

There are two kinds of locked Wheels. At a **manual** Wheel, select the available Essence; advancing the text will not bypass it. At an **automatic** Wheel, wait for the sequence to finish. The [six locked Wheels](../reference/interventions.md#gluttony-shard-wheels) include both kinds. At an optional Wheel, you can advance the text without selecting an Essence.

## Where should I save to replay other outcomes? {#save-for-replay}

Keep a manual save at the start of a recognizable scene, then use separate slots before important choices. This is usually more reliable than saving just as the Wheel appears. See the [warehouse choices and save advice](choices.qmd#warehouse) for a specific example. Some Wheels also stop the game's skip mode when they appear.

## Why is a character's Memory Codex entry still locked after I've met them? {#memorium-unlock}

Of the 41 Public 15.0 entries, 35 require you to click a highlighted character, place, event or faction name in the story. Seeing the character or reading the passage alone does not unlock the entry. The other six unlock automatically during specific scenes.

Check that you have reached the relevant scene or branch, then use the [Memory Codex & Sprite Viewer Checklist](../collectibles/memorium.md#memorium) to see whether the entry requires a click or unlocks automatically.
