This page records additions and corrections to the guide. Game release dates and guide update dates are listed separately.

## Public 15.0 · Guide update {#public-15}

**Guide updated on 10 November 2026.**

The game’s 9 November 2026 public release added main-story content and illustrations. See [Sleth’s official Public 15 announcement](https://sleths.itch.io/demons-within/devlog/1765179/demons-within-update-15-is-now-public).

- [Key Choices](guide/choices.qmd) picks up where the previous version ended. The [Full Choice Index](reference/interventions.md) and [FAQ](guide/faq.md) explain when to act and when to wait at locked Wheels.
- [CG Scenes](collectibles/cg.md) lists where to see the new chapter’s illustrations. The [Memory Codex & Sprite Viewer Checklist](collectibles/memorium.md) explains what changed between versions.
- Site search adds links to the new chapter and Viewer entry while keeping existing scene links and entries from the previous version.

## Public 14.6 · First guide edition {#public-14-6}

The game was released publicly on 4 September 2026. See [Sleth’s official Public 14.6 announcement](https://sleths.itch.io/demons-within/devlog/1652147/demons-within-update-15-out-on-patreon-and-update-14-out-for-the-public-too).

The guide launched and received the following updates.

- **20 September 2026.** The Chinese and English guides first went live on [GitHub Pages](https://github.com/yangtaomijian/demons-within-fvn-guide/actions/runs/35519393617), covering main-story choices, Wheel inputs, CGs, the Memory Codex and Sprite Viewer.
- **23 September 2026.** A [site search update](https://github.com/yangtaomijian/demons-within-fvn-guide/actions/runs/35813525032) improved results for scene names, checklist entries and common aliases.
- **25 September 2026.** The site moved to its current custom domain. See the [domain migration deployment](https://github.com/yangtaomijian/demons-within-fvn-guide/actions/runs/36154332548).
