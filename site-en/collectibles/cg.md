This page covers nine CG / special illustration groups in the **Public 14.6 main story** of *Demons Within*, organized by recognizable scenes and their viewing requirements.

The current version has no official gallery; these are categories used by this guide. Visual and story spoilers follow.

| Scene group | Where it appears | Main requirement |
|---|---|---|
| [First Night](#first-night) | The first night after recovery | Compare Lust and no intervention at the final Wheel |
| [Lust Transformation](#lust-transformation) | The first warehouse fight | Complete the sequence that continues the main story |
| [Warehouse Bad Ending](#bad-ending-1) | After capture in the warehouse | Choose Lust after capture, then finish the locked sequence |

## First Night {#first-night}

These images appear on Resnick's first night sharing a bedroom with Edwin during his recovery. After two locked tutorials, a dual Wheel determines who takes the lead and changes the accompanying text. The optional Wheel near the end determines the two main visual versions: **Lust** leads to a more intense ending, while advancing without intervening gives a gentler one.

The two versions are mutually exclusive. To see both, replay from before that final optional Wheel. You do not need to replay both sides of the earlier dual Wheel just for the main CGs. See [the first night after recovery](../guide/choices.qmd#first-night) for the full sequence.

## Lust Transformation {#lust-transformation}

During the first warehouse fight, Gunther and his companions surround Resnick. Choose **Lust** at both optional escape opportunities, then complete four locked Lust actions to see the normal transformation. Not intervening at either of the first two Wheels leads to capture.

The transformation's stages play consecutively in the same scene, on the path that continues the main story. See [the warehouse walkthrough](../guide/choices.qmd#warehouse) or [the escape opportunities](../reference/interventions.md#warehouse-escape) for the exact moments.

## Bad Ending 1 {#bad-ending-1}

This group belongs to the ending after capture in the warehouse. Leave either escape opportunity alone to have Resnick subdued. At the separate Wheel after capture, choose **Lust**, then complete the locked actions that follow to reach this sequence.

Its four main stages appear in order on the same ending path. Not intervening after capture leads to a different, shorter ending without the full group. To compare the normal transformation and both capture outcomes, replay from a save before the warehouse; see [the warehouse walkthrough](../guide/choices.qmd#warehouse).

## Sloth Reveal {#sloth-reveal}

After the day off, Resnick encounters two cloaked figures near the Watch House, and the black wolf corners him. Choose **Lust** when the wolf attacks to avoid the immediate ending and continue toward the Sloth awakening. The reveal plays at the start of that sequence.

The reveal's main stages play in sequence along the same branch. See [the black wolf ambush and Sloth awakening](../guide/choices.qmd#black-wolf).

## Sloth Choke {#sloth-choke}

Later in the same Sloth sequence, the confrontation escalates and Ludwig's enormous form pins Resnick to the bed. The artwork changes as the scene progresses.

Continue through the seven locked stages after Sloth Reveal to see this group in the same playthrough: three Sloth stages advance automatically and four Lust choices require input. No separate replay is needed for this group.

## Officer Bath {#officer-bath}

The layered images begin when Garret, Brigham and Resnick are together in the public baths. The first Wheel determines who stays: no intervention leaves Brigham with Resnick, while **Sloth** sends Brigham away and leaves Garret. Both branches use the same image group, with different expressions, poses and subsequent presentation.

Either normal branch shows the group. To compare the two characters' versions, replay from before the first Sloth opportunity.

Garret's branch later has a usable Lust choice. The Lust option on Brigham's branch is unavailable in the current version and only displays a future-update message. See [the officers' bath](../guide/choices.qmd#officer-bath) for the full choices.

## Campfire Conversation {#campfire-conversation}

This special illustration appears during Day of Reprieve, as the group eats, drinks and talks around a campfire at dusk. It appears along the shared main story with no extra prerequisites.

## Garret Asleep {#garret-asleep}

While drinking in Garret's office, choose **Sloth** at the three-way choice, then use **Sloth** on Garret again. Once he falls asleep, the first two images appear consecutively. Choose **Lust** at the later Wheel to see the third.

All three can be seen in one sequence in Public 14.6. You do not need to reload just for these images. Comparing the no-intervention text or other main dialogue branches still requires an earlier save. See [Garret's office](../guide/choices.qmd#garret-office) for prerequisites and save advice.

## Gluttony Reveal {#gluttony-reveal}

This group appears at the current end of Public 14.6. Earlier choices change who Resnick talks to during the tavern scenes, but all versions lead to the same reveal after he leaves. No further Essence choice is needed at the end.

The main visual stages play in sequence, with no reload needed. Edwin transforms into a huge, tiger-like form associated with Gluttony. The current version ends immediately afterward.
