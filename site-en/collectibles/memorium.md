This page covers the Memory Codex and Sprite Viewer unlocks in the **Public 14.6** main story of *Demons Within*. The Codex holds character, event, environment and faction entries; the Sprite Viewer separately records expressions, outfits and special forms. This guide does not reproduce the Codex text or game assets, but it does contain story spoilers.

[Memory Codex](#memorium) · [Sprite Viewer](#sprite-viewer)

## Memory Codex {#memorium}

The main story has **42 Memory Codex entries**: 14 characters, 11 events, 10 environments and seven factions. Of these, 36 require you to **click** the highlighted term in the story; six unlock automatically.

Open **Memorium → Memory Codex** in the game, check the category, page and missing slot, then select the matching entry below. Each page has **3 columns × 4 rows**, filled left to right, then top to bottom. “—” means there is no entry in that slot.

### Easy-to-Miss Branches {#memorium-missables}

- **Six entries require Bear Day:** Jen, Heirdall Library, The Marshlands, Red-eyes, Resnick's Favorite Book and Prank on Edwin.
- **Prank on Edwin and Ludwig's later-interaction Viewer states need separate replays:** leave any of the first three table opportunities alone to click Prank on Edwin during the conversation after they return to reading. Lust at all three opens the later interaction and its Viewer states. Once there, neither of the final two choices can return you to the reading scene containing Prank on Edwin. Replay from before the first table opportunity.
- **Three entries require Tiger Day:** Helen, Jayden and The Weary Soldier. Boris appears earlier on Tiger Day, but his name is clickable again in the later shared story, so his entry is not exclusive to Tiger Day.
- To compare Bear Day and Tiger Day, reload a save from before choosing Edwin's or Ludwig's day off. See [the day-off branches](../guide/choices.qmd#day-off) for the choices and save advice.
- **Exalted Donations** also depends on Sam's turn: reach his question, don't intervene, then click the term. All three steps are required.

Not yet tested in play: whether newly unlocked Codex and Viewer records survive a restart, how the list refreshes and displays after upgrading 14.0 records and restarting, and the steps for opening the Viewer.

## Sprite Viewer {#sprite-viewer}

This page lists **23 groups of Sprite Viewer variations**. Twenty-two can be unlocked through normal play in the Public 14.6 main story; the remaining group has no known normal main-story unlock. Each group may include several expressions, outfits or forms, and unlocks separately from the character's Codex entry.

To open the Viewer, unlock the character's Memory Codex entry, open their profile and click the eye icon.

Viewer states unlock automatically at the story points below; you do not need to click highlighted words. See [known limitations](#memorium-limit) for what remains untested.
